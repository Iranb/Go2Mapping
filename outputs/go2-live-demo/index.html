    Navigation limits and operation

    Review walking legs and turns before starting. The robot stops at each corner before turning. Targets must be within 4 m and total travel within 6 m. Walking commands follow measured displacement; arrival requires a settled stop within 20 cm of the target. The planner selects a nearby reachable endpoint and favors fewer turns. If walking or turning is blocked, it stops, waits for fresh scans and replans from the measured pose. A temporary endpoint allows staged progress when the full route is unverified; it does not count as final arrival. After a stop, use Replan target and review the preview before restarting. Select a new target after reconnection because the coordinate frame may change. Use on level ground with an operator present; cables, glass and low objects may be missed.
